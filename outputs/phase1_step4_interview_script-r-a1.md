# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last wedding or event you organized. Walk me through the process from the moment the client hired you until the event day.  
   _Why:_ To understand their actual workflow and identify where the complexity lies without suggesting a solution.
2. What tools or systems are you currently using to keep track of vendors, timelines, and client requests?  
   _Why:_ To identify the current 'status quo' and see if they are already using digital tools (like Excel, WhatsApp, or Notion) or manual methods.
3. Can you show me or describe a time when a vendor detail or a planning task was missed or went wrong? What happened?  
   _Why:_ To uncover specific pain points and the actual cost of disorganization (stress, money lost, reputation damage).
4. How much time do you spend each week specifically on administrative tasks like updating checklists or following up with vendors?  
   _Why:_ To quantify the time-drain and validate if the problem is significant enough to warrant a paid solution.
5. What have you already tried to do to make your planning process more organized? Did you buy any software, hire an assistant, or use specific templates?  
   _Why:_ To gauge their willingness to pay and see if they have already invested money/effort into solving this problem.
6. When you are communicating with clients or vendors in Egypt, how do you currently share updates or documents with them?  
   _Why:_ To understand the communication ecosystem and see if a Notion-based solution fits into their existing habits.
7. What is the most frustrating part of managing multiple weddings at the same time?  
   _Why:_ To identify the highest-value feature to build (e.g., is it the vendor tracking or the timeline management?)
