## Reasons

**Gamified Habit Tracker**: A mobile app that uses streaks, badges, and points to encourage daily habit consistency.
- skills: The founder has no programming or design experience required to build a mobile app.
- budget: A 0 USD budget is insufficient to cover professional development or app store fees.
- time: 2 hours per week is not enough time to learn programming and develop a mobile application.
- values: The idea can be built without ads or manipulative design to align with founder values.

**Habit Coaching Newsletter**: A weekly email service providing structured habit prompts and psychological tips for consistency.
- skills: Teaching experience suggests the founder can effectively structure and deliver educational content.
- budget: A newsletter can be launched using free email service tiers, fitting the 0 USD budget.
- time: Writing one email per week is achievable within 2 hours.
- values: A text-based newsletter can easily maintain privacy and avoid manipulative design.

**Digital Habit Journal Templates**: Downloadable PDF or Notion templates designed for manual habit logging and reward tracking.
- skills: ASSUMPTION: Requires basic layout skills which are lower barrier than programming, though design experience is still lacking.
- budget: ASSUMPTION: Creating digital files requires no upfront capital, fitting the 0 USD budget.
- time: Designing templates may take more than 2 hours per week initially, but is more manageable than coding.
- values: Static templates are inherently private and free from ads or manipulative software loops.
