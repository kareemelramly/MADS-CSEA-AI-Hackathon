## Reasons

**Gamified Habit Tracker**: A mobile app using streaks and badges to encourage daily habit consistency.
- skills: The founder has no programming or design experience required to build a mobile app.
- budget: A 0 USD budget is insufficient to cover professional development or app store fees.
- time: 2 hours per week is not enough time to learn programming and build a mobile application.
- values: The idea can be built to prioritize privacy and avoid manipulative design as requested.

**Curated Habit Newsletter**: A weekly email service providing privacy-focused habit tips and encouragement without complex software.
- skills: Teaching skills translate well to communicating concepts via email.
- budget: Email newsletters can be launched for free using existing platforms.
- time: Writing one email per week is achievable within 2 hours.
- values: A text-based newsletter can easily adhere to privacy and non-manipulative values.

**Habit-Building Digital Guide**: A downloadable PDF or ebook providing structured habit-building frameworks and manual tracking templates.
- skills: The founder's teaching experience is highly applicable to creating educational content.
- budget: Creating a digital guide can be done with 0 USD using free tools.
- time: 2 hours per week allows for slow but steady content creation.
- values: A static guide inherently avoids ads and manipulative app design.
