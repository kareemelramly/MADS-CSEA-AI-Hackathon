## Reasons

**Gamified Habit Tracker App**: A mobile app using streaks and badges to gamify daily habit tracking.
- skills: The founder has no programming or design experience required to build a mobile app.
- budget: Building a mobile app typically requires more than 0 USD for development tools or hosting.
- time: Two hours per week is insufficient to develop and maintain a mobile application.
- values: The idea of a simple gamified app can align with privacy and no-ad values if designed intentionally.

**Habit Coaching Newsletter/Guide**: A text-based digital guide or weekly email series focused on habit formation and productivity systems.
- skills: The founder's teaching background is highly transferable to creating instructional content.
- budget: Can be launched using free email marketing platforms and zero initial capital.
- time: Writing a weekly guide is achievable within a 2-hour weekly window.
- values: Text-based content is inherently low-clutter and can easily avoid manipulative design.

**Habit Tracking Digital Templates**: Pre-designed, privacy-focused digital planners or spreadsheets for habit logging and streak visualization.
- skills: Requires design skills which the founder lacks, but is less technically demanding than an app.
- budget: Can be created and distributed with 0 USD using existing digital tools.
- time: Creating templates is a finite task that can be managed within 2 hours per week.
- values: Templates can easily be designed to be ad-free and respect user privacy.
