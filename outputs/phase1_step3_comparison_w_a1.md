## Reasons

**Gamified Habit Tracker**: A mobile app using streaks and badges to encourage daily habit consistency.
- skills: The founder has no programming or design experience required to build a mobile app.
- budget: A 0 USD budget is insufficient to cover professional development or app store fees.
- time: 2 hours per week is not enough time to develop and test a mobile application.
- values: ASSUMPTION: Gamification via streaks and badges can sometimes lean into manipulative design patterns which conflicts with stated values.

**Curated Habit Newsletter**: A weekly email service providing low-pressure habit tips and psychological encouragement without complex software.
- skills: The founder's teaching background suggests ability to communicate concepts, though technical setup is needed.
- budget: A newsletter can be launched using free tiers of existing email platforms.
- time: Writing a weekly newsletter is achievable within 2 hours per week.
- values: A text-based newsletter can easily adhere to privacy and no-ad values.

**Digital Habit Workbook**: A downloadable PDF guide with structured daily prompts and reward tracking for manual use.
- skills: ASSUMPTION: Requires basic layout skills which the founder currently lacks, but is easier than coding.
- budget: Creating a digital document can be done with zero financial investment.
- time: Designing a single workbook can be managed within the limited weekly time frame.
- values: A static digital product naturally avoids manipulative design and ads.
