## Reasons

**Gamified Habit Tracker App**: A mobile app using streaks and badges to gamify daily habit tracking.
- skills: The founder has no programming or design experience required for mobile app development.
- budget: Building a mobile app typically requires more than 0 USD for development tools or hosting.
- time: Two hours per week is insufficient to develop and maintain a mobile application.
- values: Gamification can align with rewards, but must be carefully designed to avoid manipulative patterns.

**Privacy-First Habit Newsletter**: A weekly email service providing habit-building prompts and productivity tips without tracking user data.
- skills: Teaching experience translates well to content creation and structured guidance.
- budget: Email newsletters can be started with zero budget using free tiers of existing platforms.
- time: Writing one weekly email is achievable within a 2-hour weekly window.
- values: Newsletters can easily be ad-free and respect user privacy by not requiring app permissions.

**Digital Habit Workbook**: A downloadable PDF guide or digital planner for manual habit logging and streak visualization.
- skills: Requires basic layout skills which are more accessible than programming.
- budget: Creating a digital document can be done with free design tools.
- time: Designing a high-quality workbook may take more than 2 hours per week initially.
- values: A static document is inherently private and contains no manipulative software design.
