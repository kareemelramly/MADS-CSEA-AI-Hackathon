# Phase 2 · Step 1 · Lean Canvas

## Problem

Existing habit trackers are often cluttered with unnecessary features and lack sufficient short-term rewards for consistency. Users also face privacy concerns and a lack of systems that account for consistency without being erased by a single missed day. [S009](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47231047) [S024](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40147989) [S047](https://github.com/Tobiashopf/weekforge/issues/9)

## Customer Segments

Adults, students, and professionals seeking productivity tools that prioritize privacy and simplicity. [S043](https://github.com/Lparedes14/sharkbyte_hackathon/issues/2) [S044](https://github.com/Lparedes14/habit-tracker-test/issues/1)

## Unique Value Proposition

A privacy-first, ad-free habit tracker that uses gamified streaks and badges to reward consistent behavior without manipulative design. [S003](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47911543) [S005](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47892056) [S012](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47874985)

## Solution

A mobile app featuring repeatable activity logging, points, rewards, and a month-view visualization of streaks. [S056](https://github.com/buwaneka-k/Habit-Tracker_001/issues/1) [S059](https://github.com/gh01wzx/focusflow/issues/108)

## Channels

Organic growth through privacy-focused tech communities, app store optimization, and word-of-mouth among productivity enthusiasts. (ASSUMPTION)

## Revenue Streams

A one-time digital purchase for a premium version that unlocks advanced features or custom themes. [S003](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47911543) [S012](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47874985)

## Cost Structure

Minimal costs related to app store developer fees and potential hosting for cloud-syncing options, though local storage is prioritized. [S014](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41647328)

## Key Metrics

Daily active users (DAU), streak retention rates, and the conversion rate from free to premium users. (ASSUMPTION)

## Unfair Advantage

A unique commitment to non-manipulative, privacy-centric design that differentiates from data-hungry competitors. [S005](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47892056) [S014](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41647328)
