# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last wedding you planned or attended. Walk me through the process from the moment the engagement was announced until the wedding day.  
   _Why:_ To understand their natural workflow and identify where the actual complexity lies without suggesting a tool.
2. How are you currently keeping track of all the different vendors (venues, catering, photographers, etc.) and their contracts?  
   _Why:_ To see if they are using existing tools (Excel, WhatsApp, paper) and to identify the friction points in their current method.
3. Can you show me or describe the specific system you use to make sure no tasks or deadlines are missed?  
   _Why:_ To understand the level of organization required and whether they are already solving the problem manually.
4. Tell me about a time when something went wrong with a vendor or a planning detail. How did you find out, and how did you manage it?  
   _Why:_ To identify the 'pain points'—the specific moments where a tracker or checklist would have provided actual value.
5. What tools or services have you already paid for to help with the wedding planning process?  
   _Why:_ To gauge their willingness to spend money on organization and to see what competitors (apps, planners, or consultants) they value.
6. How much time do you spend each week specifically on administrative tasks like updating lists or checking vendor statuses?  
   _Why:_ To quantify the time-saving potential of the product, which helps in determining the value proposition.
7. When you look for help or tools online for wedding planning, where do you usually go first?  
   _Why:_ To understand their information-seeking behavior and where the most effective marketing channels might be in the Egyptian market.
