# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last wedding you planned or attended. Walk me through how you kept track of all the moving parts.  
   _Why:_ To understand their current workflow and identify if they are actually using a system or just relying on memory/chaos.
2. What tools or methods are you currently using to manage vendor communications and checklists?  
   _Why:_ To identify the specific tools (WhatsApp, Excel, paper, etc.) they rely on and see if there is a gap in their current setup.
3. Can you describe a specific time when a vendor detail or a task was missed or went wrong? What happened?  
   _Why:_ To uncover the actual pain points and the 'cost' of their current disorganized system.
4. How much time do you spend each week specifically updating your planning documents or trackers?  
   _Why:_ To quantify the time-drain and validate if 'efficiency' is a problem worth paying to solve.
5. What have you already tried to do to make your planning process more organized? (e.g., buying planners, hiring assistants, new software)  
   _Why:_ To see if they have a history of spending money to solve this problem, which validates demand.
6. How do you currently share updates or checklists with your clients or team members?  
   _Why:_ To understand the collaboration requirements and whether a digital tool like Notion would actually fit their communication style.
7. When you look for new ways to manage your business or event, where do you usually go to find solutions?  
   _Why:_ To identify the best marketing channels and where the target audience seeks inspiration or tools.
