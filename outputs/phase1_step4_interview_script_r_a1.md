# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last wedding or event you organized. Walk me through the process from the moment the client hired you to the day of the event.  
   _Why:_ To understand their actual workflow and identify where the 'chaos' or manual work happens without suggesting a solution.
2. How are you currently keeping track of vendor details, contracts, and payment schedules?  
   _Why:_ To discover their current tools (Excel, WhatsApp, paper, etc.) and see if they are actually solving the problem or just managing it.
3. Tell me about a time when a vendor detail or a task was missed or went wrong. What happened and how did you find out?  
   _Why:_ To identify the specific pain points and the high cost of failure, which validates the need for a better tracking system.
4. What tools or systems have you already paid for or spent time setting up to manage your wedding planning process?  
   _Why:_ To gauge their willingness to spend money on organization and to see if they are already investing in digital solutions.
5. How much time do you spend each week specifically on administrative tasks like updating checklists or following up with vendors?  
   _Why:_ To quantify the time-drain, which helps in calculating the value proposition of a time-saving template.
6. When you are looking for new ways to organize your work, where do you usually go to find solutions or inspiration?  
   _Why:_ To understand their information-seeking behavior and identify where to market the product (e.g., specific Facebook groups, Instagram, or Pinterest).
7. What is the most frustrating part about the current way you manage your client's wedding data?  
   _Why:_ To uncover the emotional driver behind the need for a new tool, which is essential for messaging and design.
