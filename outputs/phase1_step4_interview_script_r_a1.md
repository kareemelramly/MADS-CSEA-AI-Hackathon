# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last wedding or event you organized. Walk me through how you kept track of all the moving parts.  
   _Why:_ To understand their current workflow and identify if they actually have a complex organization problem or if it's simple enough to manage without tools.
2. What specific tools or methods are you currently using to manage vendor communications and checklists?  
   _Why:_ To identify the existing 'stack' (WhatsApp, Excel, paper, etc.) and see if there is a gap between what they use and what they need.
3. Can you describe a time when something went wrong with a vendor or a deadline was missed? How did you find out, and how did you handle it?  
   _Why:_ To uncover the specific pain points and 'expensive' mistakes that a tracker could have prevented.
4. How much time do you spend each week updating your planning documents or chasing vendor information?  
   _Why:_ To quantify the time-cost of their current manual process, which helps determine the value of an automated/structured template.
5. What have you already tried to do to make your planning process more organized? Did you buy any software, books, or templates?  
   _Why:_ To gauge actual willingness to spend money. If they haven't spent money to solve the problem yet, they likely won't buy a template.
6. When you are looking for new tools or ways to organize your work, where do you usually go to find them?  
   _Why:_ To understand their information-seeking behavior and identify the best marketing channels for the Egyptian market.
7. How do you currently share information or progress updates with your clients or family members?  
   _Why:_ To see if the 'collaboration' aspect of Notion is a necessary feature or if they prefer one-way communication like PDF exports.
