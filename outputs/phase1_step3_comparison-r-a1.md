## Reasons

**Notion Wedding Planner Pack**: A set of Notion templates including a planning checklist and vendor tracker specifically for the Egyptian market.
- skills: The founder is a Notion power user and has direct experience organizing weddings.
- budget: A 200 USD budget is sufficient for creating and distributing digital Notion templates.
- time: 10 hours per week is adequate for template design and marketing.
- values: The product is a utility-focused tool which aligns with the value of providing real utility over hype.

**Visual Wedding Moodboard Kit**: A digital asset pack of graphic design elements and templates for creating visual wedding boards.
- skills: Leverages the founder's graphic design skills to address visual organization needs.
- budget: Low overhead costs for digital graphic assets.
- time: Design work can be managed within the 10-hour weekly limit.
- values: Focuses on creative utility rather than hype.

**Wedding Vendor Directory PDF/E-book**: A curated, high-quality guide of vetted Egyptian wedding vendors to avoid low-quality SEO sites.
- skills: Requires research and organization skills rather than technical Notion/design skills.
- budget: Minimal cost to compile and distribute a digital guide.
- time: Vetting vendors may require more time than template creation.
- values: Directly addresses the pain point of low-quality, SEO-driven industry tools.
