# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last time you tried to start or maintain a new daily habit.  
   _Why:_ To understand if they actually attempt to change behavior and what their natural starting process looks like.
2. What tools, apps, or physical items are you currently using to track your habits or routines?  
   _Why:_ To identify existing competitors and see if they are already spending time or money on a solution.
3. Walk me through how you felt the last time you missed a day of a habit you were trying to build.  
   _Why:_ To uncover the emotional friction and the specific 'pain point' of failure without asking if they would like a gamified solution.
4. How much time do you spend each day or week managing your habit trackers or planning your routines?  
   _Why:_ To gauge the level of commitment and the actual 'cost' of their current workflow.
5. What have you tried in the past to stay motivated when a habit gets difficult or boring?  
   _Why:_ To see if they have already sought out 'rewards' or 'gamification' on their own, which validates the need for your idea.
6. When looking at the apps you currently use, what are some things about their design or data handling that you actively avoid?  
   _Why:_ To validate the demand for privacy and non-manipulative design through their past negative experiences rather than their opinions.
7. Can you show me (or describe) a specific instance where a notification or a digital reward actually helped you complete a task?  
   _Why:_ To see if 'streaks' or 'badges' actually work in real life for this specific user, rather than asking if they 'would like' them.
