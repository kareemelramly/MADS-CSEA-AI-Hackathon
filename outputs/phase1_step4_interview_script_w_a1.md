# Phase 1 · Step 4 · Interview script (Mom Test)

Ask about past behaviour only. No answers are invented; record real answers yourself.

1. Tell me about the last time you tried to start a new daily habit or routine.  
   _Why:_ To understand if they actually attempt to change behavior and what their natural starting process looks like.
2. What specific tools or methods are you currently using to keep track of your habits?  
   _Why:_ To identify existing competitors (analog or digital) and see what they are currently willing to use.
3. Walk me through how you managed your habits yesterday or earlier today.  
   _Why:_ To see if habit tracking is a real, recurring part of their life or just a theoretical interest.
4. When you've used habit-tracking apps in the past, what made you stop using them?  
   _Why:_ To uncover pain points like notification fatigue, complexity, or 'guilt' mechanics without asking for opinions on my specific idea.
5. How much time or money have you spent on tools or resources to help you stay disciplined in the past?  
   _Why:_ To gauge their actual commitment and willingness to pay for self-improvement solutions.
6. Tell me about a time you felt frustrated by a digital product's design or how it handled your data.  
   _Why:_ To validate if 'privacy' and 'no manipulative design' are actual pain points or just things people say they like.
7. What is the most difficult part about staying consistent with your current routines?  
   _Why:_ To identify the core problem the app needs to solve (e.g., forgetfulness vs. lack of motivation) so I don't build unnecessary features.
